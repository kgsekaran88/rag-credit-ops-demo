# Meridian Bank — Credit Operations Circular 2024 (SUPERSEDED)

Personal-loan EMI hardship relief after involuntary job loss requires an in-branch
request and Branch Manager approval.

Maximum relief period is 30 days.
There is no self-serve digital path for job-loss EMI relief.

Applies to retail personal loans under Credit Ops Circular 2024-03.
