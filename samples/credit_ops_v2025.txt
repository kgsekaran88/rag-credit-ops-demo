# Meridian Bank — Credit Operations Circular 2025 (CURRENT)

Personal-loan EMI hardship relief after involuntary job loss may be requested in the
mobile app with an employer job-loss certificate.

Maximum relief period is 90 days.
Accounts with EMI ≤ ₹25,000 and 12 months good standing are auto-eligible for digital approval.

Applies to retail personal loans under Credit Ops Circular 2025-06.
